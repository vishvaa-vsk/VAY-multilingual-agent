## Eligibility & Offer Terms

- **New-connection offers** (e.g. first month free, discounted activation) apply only to
  numbers with no prior Nexatel account in the last 90 days on the same Aadhaar/KYC ID.
- **Plan upgrade eligibility**: Any active prepaid or postpaid customer can upgrade at any
  time; the new plan's benefits apply immediately with a pro-rata charge/credit adjustment
  (postpaid) or immediate balance deduction (prepaid).
- **Plan downgrade eligibility**: Postpaid customers may downgrade once per billing cycle;
  downgrade takes effect from the *next* billing cycle, not immediately, to avoid mid-cycle
  service disruption. Prepaid customers may switch plans on their next recharge freely.
- **Family plan eligibility**: Up to 4 connections can be linked under Postpaid Family/Pro
  plans, all billed to one primary account holder; each linked number must complete its own
  KYC.
- **Student/Youth plan eligibility**: Youth Unlimited requires age 18–25 verified via a valid
  student ID or Aadhaar DOB at activation; re-verified annually.
- **Loyalty/renewal offers**: Customers active for 12+ continuous months are eligible for a
  loyalty discount of 10% on annual prepaid recharges or one free add-on of choice per
  quarter, auto-applied — no separate application needed.
- **Offer stacking**: Only one promotional discount can be applied per transaction; loyalty
  discounts and new-connection offers cannot be combined.
- All offer prices are subject to change with 7 days' prior notice via SMS/app notification
  per TRAI's Tariff Order requirements; existing active recharges are honored at the price
  paid until their validity ends.

## Comparing Plans — Guidance for the Assistant

When a customer asks to compare plans or find the "best" plan, the assistant should ask (or
infer from context) their primary need — data-heavy, voice-heavy, family sharing, or budget —
and recommend from the tables above rather than inventing a plan. If a customer's stated
budget or need doesn't match any current plan exactly, recommend the closest matching plan and
clearly state the difference (e.g. "the closest match is Prepaid Plus at ₹399, which gives you
1 GB/day more than what you asked for").
