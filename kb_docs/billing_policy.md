# Nexatel Billing & Payments Policy

Nexatel Communications Ltd. ("Nexatel") is a licensed telecom operator offering prepaid,
postpaid, broadband, and DTH services. This document is the authoritative Billing-Policy
knowledge base for the Nexatel customer-care voice assistant's Billing & Payments Agent.
It covers tariff rules, billing cycles, late-fee and roaming policy, the charge glossary,
and refund rules. All figures are in Indian Rupees (INR) and inclusive/exclusive of GST as
noted.

## Billing Cycle Basics

### Postpaid Billing Cycle
Postpaid Nexatel accounts are billed on a fixed monthly cycle assigned at the time of
activation (e.g. 1st-to-end-of-month, or a mid-month cycle such as 14th-to-13th). The bill
is generated within 24 hours of the cycle close and is available in the MyNexatel app, on
nexatel.in, and via SMS summary. Postpaid customers get a **21-day payment window** from the
bill generation date before the account is marked overdue.

### Prepaid Recharge & Validity
Prepaid accounts do not receive monthly bills; instead, customers recharge with a plan that
has a fixed validity (e.g. 28, 56, 84, or 365 days). Nexatel sends a low-balance / plan-expiry
reminder via SMS 3 days before expiry. Prepaid balance and validity can be checked any time by
saying "check my balance" to the assistant or dialing *123#.

### Broadband/DTH Billing Cycle
Broadband and DTH accounts are billed monthly in advance, with the bill generated on the
account's activation anniversary date each month. Payment is due within 15 days of bill
generation.

## Late Fees & Non-Payment Policy

- **Postpaid**: If payment is not received within the 21-day window, a **late payment fee of
  ₹50 flat + 1.5% of the outstanding amount per month** is applied. Outgoing services (calls,
  SMS, data) are suspended 5 days after the due date if unpaid; incoming calls continue for a
  further 15 days ("grace period") before full suspension. Full disconnection and number
  recycling risk begins after 90 consecutive days of non-payment, per TRAI's mandated
  disconnection timeline.
- **Broadband/DTH**: A late fee of **₹100 flat** applies if payment is not received within 15
  days of bill generation. Service is suspended 7 days after the due date if still unpaid.
- **Prepaid**: There is no "late fee" concept since prepaid is pay-in-advance; when a plan
  expires without renewal, outgoing services stop immediately and incoming calls/SMS continue
  for 30 days ("incoming-only grace period") before the number becomes eligible for recycling
  under TRAI rules (typically 90 days total inactivity).

## Roaming Policy

### Domestic Roaming
All Nexatel prepaid and postpaid plans include **free domestic roaming** — there is no extra
charge for using your Nexatel number outside your home telecom circle within India. Standard
plan rates for calls, SMS, and data apply exactly as they would at home.

### International Roaming (IR)
International roaming is NOT included in regular plans and must be activated separately via
an IR pack before travel:
- **IR Lite Pack** — ₹499, valid 10 days: 100 MB data/day, 100 incoming minutes free,
  outgoing calls to India at ₹10/min, outgoing calls to local country at ₹15/min.
- **IR Value Pack** — ₹1,499, valid 10 days: 1 GB data/day, unlimited incoming calls,
  outgoing calls to India at ₹5/min, outgoing SMS ₹5/SMS.
- **IR Unlimited Pack** — ₹2,999, valid 10 days: 2 GB/day high-speed data (then throttled),
  unlimited incoming and outgoing calls to India and the visited country.
- Without an active IR pack, using data/calls abroad is charged at default International
  Roaming rates, which are significantly higher (data ₹500/MB, calls ₹100/min) — customers
  MUST be advised to activate an IR pack before international travel.
- IR packs must be activated at least 2 hours before departure via the MyNexatel app,
  nexatel.in, or by calling the IR activation hotline.

## Charge Glossary

| Charge Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fixed Charge / Rental** | The base monthly subscription fee for a postpaid or broadband plan, charged regardless of usage. |
| **Usage Charge** | Charges for calls/SMS/data beyond what is included free in the plan (pay-per-use, "PPU" rate applies). |
| **Late Payment Fee** | Penalty applied when a bill is not paid within its due window (see Late Fees section). |
| **Roaming Surcharge** | Extra charge for using services outside the home circle without an active roaming/IR pack. |
| **Value Added Service (VAS) Charge** | Recurring charge for an opted-in add-on service such as caller tune, OTT bundle, or insurance add-on. |
| **GST (Goods & Services Tax)** | 18% tax applied on all telecom charges as per Government of India regulation; shown as a separate line item on every bill. |
| **Processing Fee** | A one-time fee (₹25–₹50) charged for certain manual actions like duplicate SIM issuance or address change. |
| **Security Deposit** | A refundable deposit collected at postpaid activation (typically equal to one month's rental), adjusted or refunded on plan downgrade or account closure. |
| **Pro-rata Charge** | A partial charge/credit calculated for the exact number of days a plan was active, used when a plan is changed mid-cycle. |
| **Rounding-off Adjustment** | A charge/credit of a few paise to round the final bill to the nearest rupee. |

## Explaining a High Bill — Common Causes

When a customer asks "why is my bill so high," the most common causes, in order of frequency,
are:
1. **Data/call usage beyond the plan's free limit** — pay-per-use (PPU) rates apply beyond
   the included quota (e.g. ₹0.50/min for extra voice, ₹10/GB for extra data beyond FUP).
2. **An active VAS/add-on subscription** the customer forgot about (caller tune, OTT bundle,
   insurance) — these auto-renew monthly unless deactivated.
3. **International roaming usage without an active IR pack.**
4. **A mid-cycle plan upgrade**, which is billed pro-rata for the remaining days of the old
   plan plus the new plan's charge from the upgrade date.
5. **GST (18%)** applied on top of the base amount, which customers sometimes read as an
   unexplained addition.
6. **A previous month's unpaid balance carried forward** plus a late payment fee.

## Refund Rules

- **Duplicate/erroneous payment**: If a customer is charged twice for the same bill (e.g. an
  auto-debit and a manual payment both went through), the duplicate amount is refunded to the
  original payment method within **5–7 working days** automatically; no complaint ticket is
  required, but one can be raised to track status.
- **Service downtime refund**: If a customer experienced a verified outage lasting more than
  24 continuous hours in their service area, they are eligible for a pro-rata credit on their
  next bill equal to the affected days' rental — this requires a complaint ticket referencing
  the outage.
- **Plan downgrade mid-cycle**: Postpaid customers who downgrade mid-cycle receive a pro-rata
  credit for the unused portion of the higher plan, applied to the next bill (not a direct
  cash refund).
- **Account closure refund**: Any remaining security deposit and unused prepaid balance is
  refunded within **15 working days** of confirmed account closure, via bank transfer to the
  registered account.
- Refunds are **never** given in cash at retail outlets; all refunds are processed digitally
  to the original payment source or registered bank account.

## Payment Methods & Payment Links

Nexatel accepts payment via: MyNexatel app (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets), nexatel.in
web portal, auto-pay/standing instruction (UPI Autopay or NACH mandate), authorized retail
outlets (cash/card), and IVR-guided card payment on the customer care line. When a payment
link is sent to a customer (via SMS/WhatsApp), it is valid for **24 hours** and can be used
only for the exact outstanding amount at the time of generation — a fresh link must be
requested if the amount changes (e.g. after further usage).

## Due Date & Reminders

Nexatel sends bill/due-date reminders via SMS and app notification: 3 days before due date,
on the due date, and 3 days after (overdue notice). Customers can also opt into WhatsApp
billing reminders via the MyNexatel app settings.
